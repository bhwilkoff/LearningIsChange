# 05.13.08

Cores 1+4:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080513-et2q8j29stb9hik6jnhs2imu53.jpg?w=1225)
- Read [Chapter 8 of Animal Farm](http://www.readprint.com/chapter-7654/George-Orwell)

- Why are the pigs new rules so far reaching?
- Why are they not satisfied with equality?
- Did the animal truly succeed in the second battle? What did they win?

- Write from the viewpoint of a pig and of another animal in describing the battle of the Windmill.
- What is different about the way that they view the battle, and the world.

Core 2:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080513-bc65i7gqfau57rsryp4k954g5j.jpg?w=1225)
- Rev-It-Up
- Finish [Chapter 9 and Start Chapter 10](http://www.readprint.com/chapter-7655/George-Orwell)

- What is going to happen to Animal Farm with Boxer’s leadership?

- Discuss the script, bringing in supplies, etc.
- Practice scenes

- Extensions:
- Finish your Utopia for this weekend.

Core 3:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080513-d2kmywt29d191fa6514cdy1uqh.jpg?w=1225)
- Read the [Temperance Reform Introduction](http://www.teachushistory.org/Temperance/forstudents.htm)

- Why was it that people drank so much in the 19th century?
- How did the temperance movement garner so much support from different groups of people?

- Answer the two questions at the end of the article according to your own -Ism:
- How should individual human beings
behave?
- How does that behavior influence others?

- Extensions:
- Finish your -Ism this weekend.
