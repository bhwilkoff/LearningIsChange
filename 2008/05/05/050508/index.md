# 05.05.08

Cores 1+4:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080421-pbqqr4yq18hr6w9te4i6jdxqb7.jpg?w=1225)
- [Read Animal Farm Chapter 7.](http://www.readprint.com/chapter-7653/George-Orwell)

- Why does everyone see/hear/smell Snowball?
- Why is it necessary to change everyone’s viewpoint of snowball by altering the past?
- Why do the dogs go after Boxer?
- Why is beasts of England really banned?
- Do the punishments at the end of Chapter 7 fit the crimes?

- Extensions:
- Finish the final draft of your Utopia for Friday.

Core 2:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080505-q5u2d3npchghrh614d5wcxcw2n.jpg?resize=593%2C395)
- Rev-it-Up
- Discuss Script, parts, costumes and crew.

- Speaking Parts
- Extras
- Camera people
- Costume creators

- Audition and Finalize Roles
- Extensions:
- Practice your part for Animal Farm Movie/Play.

Core 3:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080505-jkdmm25wwcbg1y9yqn9ax713mq.jpg?w=1225)

- Outer Circle
- Outer Circle Moderators

- Extensions:
- Prepare for debate tomorrow.
