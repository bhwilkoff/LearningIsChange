# 01.17.08

Cores 1+4:

- ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080117-mpk1gimq37juhgdkptc3h6c4mk.jpg?w=1225) (Write-on)
- What are the major flaws with More’s Utopia?

- Draw a picture of what you think could go wrong in his vision of a perfect society.

- Check-in on Personal Curriculum.
- How are the [French Utopians](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution) different in their depictions of Utopia?
- Extensions:
- What kind of ideas seem to be reoccurring in the different utopias?

Core 2:

- ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080117-khjtdayp4yu539aq374r6xmbfn.jpg?w=1225)(Write-on)
- Take Rev-It-Up Quiz.
- What would be your own personal dystopia?
- How does your personal dystopia compare to that of [Harrison Bergeron](http://instruct.westvalley.edu/lafave/hb.html)?
- Extension:

- If you haven’t finished your SAP, e-mail it to me tomorrow.

Core 3:

- ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080117-29dnghm8ujspjk89ix2gt6r7w.jpg?w=1225) (Brainstorm-on)
- Decipher the writing identity of your fellow students.
- Extension:

- Read your AR book.
