# 01.15.08

Cores 1+4:

- Write-on: How does you concept of utopia (your idea of perfection) influence your idea of existence? (How are utopia and existentialism (the questioning of existence) related?)
- Go over questions from [How the World Was Saved.](http://extension.fleck.com/?sh=10bbf4c479ff44cd373468feb2ba8a20de310125)
- Take a look at the [original concept of utopia from Thomas More.](http://books.google.com/books?id=AOX5AZdTxEIC&printsec=frontcover&dq=Thomas+More#PPA4,M1)

- What does his vision of Utopia say about his time period?

- Extension:
- Read AR Book.

Core 2:

- Rev-It-On
- What is a [smart mob?](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_mob)

- What is their take on social action?
- Are we creating Smart Mobs?

- Continue to work on Social Action Plans.
- Extensions:
- Finish SAP for Friday (Thursday for E.C.)

Core 3:

- Discuss-on: How will you make sure that each one of your writing style descriptions does not turn out the same? What will you specifically looking for to differentiate them?
- Research each blogger’s writing style from our class and collect writing style “data” for you to use on Thursday in order to decipher which anonymous writer is which.
- Extension:

- If you do not finish, please finish at home.
