- Complete survey for Core 2 Social Action Plan  [Boys](http://www.polldaddy.com/Feeds/Surveys/14959ajicsptdwjazomxgbpqt.xml)

- Write-on: Write out the longest sentence you can without making any grammatical errors.
- What kind of image grammar can you see in the [600 word sentence from Proust?](http://www.mrbauld.com/proustsent.html)  What kinds of words, punctuation, and other conventions do you see joining things together in this sentence?
- Why should you know how to string together a long sentence grammatically?

- Discuss-On: What is there difference between an idea and a belief?
- What happens when you string enough beliefs together?
- What idiologies/-isms/structures of belief exist today?  How do you think they got started?

- Extension:  Revise your long sentence with some of the skills that you learned today.
