# 02.26.08

Core 1:

- Blog-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080226-kq1qjc2p6q9k7n8dg7hbqctid1.jpg?w=1225)
- Starting to work on personal curriculum with a view toward sharing your knowledge.  How will you demonstrate that you have learned something?
- What do you still need in order to get your personal curriculum goal accomplished?
- Will others be able to learn from you?

- Work on your long-running Academy Authentic, to be shared out for commenting at the end of this week.
- Comment on the Utopian poems from [one another’s blogs](http://academyofdiscovery.com/wp/index.php/student-blogs).
- Extensions:  Continue your work on personal curriculum and academy authentics.

Core 2:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080226-fm1su2etfq77ecwc783dehhm9a.jpg?w=1225)
- Start your own Journal document (you don’t have to share it with anyone) and type up your Day 1 Journal Entry, revising it for detail as you go.
- Finalize your goals today and get your first what-if.  Choose a what-if out of the hat and react to it in a new section of your collaborative document.

- Extensions:  Continue to work on your Survival Simulations.

Core 3:

- Goal for today:  To receive feedback on your reflective writing piece from your classmates.

- How to get it done.  Post your piece to your blog and ask your friends to comment on it.
- Tell them to write about what truth or beliefs are explored in this piece.

- Goal for today:  To choose a new way to blog from the 25 listed here:
-

[![SlideShare](https://i0.wp.com/static.slideshare.net/swf/logo_embd.png?w=1225)](http://www.slideshare.net/?src=embed) | [View](http://www.slideshare.net/rohitbhargava/the-25-basic-styles-of-blogging-and-when-to-use-each-one?src=embed) | [Upload your own](http://www.slideshare.net/upload?src=embed)

- How to get it done.  Write a new post of your choosing within a new genre of blogging in order to expand what is possible on your blog and to grow our community without another assignment blogging piece.

- Extensions:  Continue to comment on the reflective pieces and finish your “new blogging” piece.
