Core 3:

- Blog-on: ![](http://www.ourtenwords.com/)[![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080326-bedrthki851x1x72q2t6cyheyn.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.ourtenwords.com/)
- Collect “Yellow Wallpapers”
- What is [framing a debate?](http://www.rockridgeinstitute.org/projects/strategic/simple_framing)
- What would have been [the great debate about feminism in the 19th century?](http://wilkoffd8.blogspot.com/2006/04/framing-debate-is-essential-to-winning.html)
- Get into groups to compete in the elite 8 for framing the debate on Women’s Suffrage. Construct a 10 word statement that frames the debate so that you cannot lose. Explain why you believe that this statement is so powerful.
- Extensions: Ensure that your statement is as potent as it can be.
