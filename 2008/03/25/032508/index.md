# 03.25.08

Cores 1+4:

- ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080325-c47itkbgsdr2cds8ac3ix8rkmb.jpg?w=1225)
- Fleshing out Origin:

- What are all of the ideas that a utopia/society can be based upon?

- What ideas were the basis for the societies in your reaction novels?
- How do your originating ideas influence every aspect of utopia?

- Start to write the story of your origin as someone would tell it to one another within your society.
- Extensions:
- Continue to explore last year’s [Utopia Wiki](http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com/) and do a review on your blog for Tomorrow.
- Finish reading your novel for Friday.

Core 2:

- Write-on: ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080325-xpp5g1ke3rj4jnq5agtks9yyd6.jpg?w=1225)
- Rev-it-Up: The Words
- Read Chapter 1 of Animal Farm:

- What are the seeds of rebellion?
- Who represents whom in the story?

- Extensions:
- Explore last year’s [Utopia Wiki](http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com/).

Core 3:

- ![](https://i0.wp.com/img.skitch.com/20080325-neawxd7ysr1x3kgthep8cuxsah.jpg?w=1225)
- Draw your “Yellow Wallpaper” as a symbolic representation of what holds you/society/men/women back from fulfilling their potential.

- Requirements:
- Some thematic color
- A symbolic pattern
- A description of the picture, odor, and figures in the wallpaper.

- Share Out and discuss “Yellow Wallpapers.”
- Extensions:
- Finish your wallpapers.
