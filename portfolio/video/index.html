Why video matters

Video has been part of my work since 2007. It started with the Learning is Change List vlogs, built on the idea that a small group of people in a community of practice can change education. It continued through the daily D.A.V.I.D. briefings and into TikTok for The Masculinity Detox. I have never cared much about production quality. I want to show up, say what I actually think, and let people see my face while I say it.

Video + Writing

The discipline of daily creation

In 2010 I wrote a question a day on the blog and kept making videos the whole time. That year taught me something I already suspected: I need to make something every day, and it has nothing to do with productivity. The blog is where I work through an idea slowly, over a lot of words. Video is where I look into a camera and say what I actually think, with no chance to revise. Both require showing up. Both reward the practice more than any one piece. Together they are the record of twenty years of learning in public.
