Why video matters

Video is the most human medium we have online. It carries tone, expression, vulnerability — things that text alone can't fully convey. For someone who believes that education is fundamentally about human connection, video is a natural extension of that belief into the digital space.

Video + Writing

Two sides of the same practice

My video work and writing are complementary. The blog is where I go deep — exploring ideas with nuance over thousands of words. Video is where I connect directly — sharing the same ideas with the immediacy and authenticity that only a face-to-camera conversation can provide. Together, they form a complete practice of public learning and reflection.
