Why video matters

Video has been part of my work since 2007. It started with the original Learning is Change List vlogs — dedicated to the idea that a small group of people can change education by being in a community of practice. It continued through daily D.A.V.I.D. briefings as civic engagement, and into TikTok for The Masculinity Detox. Across all of it, the point has never been production quality. It’s about showing up consistently and connecting directly. A camera, a thought, and the willingness to be honest — that’s the whole formula.

Video + Writing

The discipline of daily creation

In 2010, I wrote a question every single day on the blog and kept making videos at the same time. That year taught me something I already suspected: the consistency of daily creation is not a productivity hack — it’s who I am. Video and writing are two expressions of the same impulse. The blog is where I work through ideas slowly, over thousands of words. Video is where I look into a camera and say what I actually think, without the safety net of revision. Both require showing up. Both reward the practice more than any individual piece. Together, they are the complete record of twenty years of learning in public.
