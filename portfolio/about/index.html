The Person Behind the Work

About Me

I live at the intersection of education and technology. While the intersection is always busy, not everyone knows how to navigate it. I like to help folks do exactly that.

Who I Am

I'm Ben Wilkoff. I'm an educator, a builder, and a writer. I've spent over twenty years working in education — starting in a middle school English classroom and winding my way through district leadership, edtech companies, university programs, and now international higher education.

The through line has always been the same: a deep belief that technology should serve people, that learning is the most powerful form of change, and that showing up consistently to do the work matters more than any single breakthrough.

I own a typewriter and I collect Laserdiscs. I love my three children quite a bit. I create at least one new thing every day. I believe in the power of sustained effort toward important goals.

I was compelled to share my story of creating an artificial separation between technology and pedagogy to defining the connections. I love talking about where I have been, where I am going, and what it takes to frame change for success.

What I Believe

Learning is Change

It's not just the name of my blog. It's a core conviction. Real learning transforms us — it changes how we see the world, how we act in it, and who we become. That transformation is the point.

Technology Has a Purpose

Technology is not pedagogically agnostic. It was made by people, for a purpose. We have a responsibility to choose technology that was made for the right purpose — and to build technology that serves learners first.

Show Up Every Day

Whether it's writing a blog post, creating a video, or building a tool — the practice of daily creation is its own reward. Consistency compounds. Twenty years of blogging taught me that.

Build in the Open

Every project is open source. Every thought gets published. Sharing work publicly isn't just generosity — it's accountability, community building, and an invitation for others to learn alongside you.

The Masculinity Detox

The alpha male is a myth.

I founded The Masculinity Detox because suppressing our emotions can literally shorten our life spans. It's an inclusive online community platform designed to bring people together and discuss the ways in which we can eliminate toxic behavior and build healthier models of masculinity.

This work is deeply personal and deeply important. It connects my roles as an educator, a father of three, and a person who believes that vulnerability is strength.
