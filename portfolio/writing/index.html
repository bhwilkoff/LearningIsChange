Highlighted Posts

Selected writing from across two decades that captures the core ideas behind Learning is Change.

  • Aug 2006
    The 95 Theses of Progressive Teaching
    A manifesto for progressive education inspired by Martin Luther. 50+ theses on what teachers should be, from “Teachers should be the change they want to see in their schools” to “Teachers should learn.”
  • Jun 2007
    The Ripe Environment
    The 10 prerequisites for collaboration — Ben’s defining educational framework about creating spaces where people actually want to learn and grow together.
  • 2007 · 43 comments
    Safety vs. Panic
    One of the most-discussed posts in the blog’s history, exploring the tension between creating safe spaces and the productive discomfort of real learning.
  • Dec 2010
    Question 365 of 365: What is enough?
    The final entry in a year-long project of writing one question every day — 365 questions about education, technology, and community.
  • Feb 2022
    Why I Left Public Education (and then came back)
    “Scale is a seductive myth.” A reflection on leaving classroom teaching for EdTech, the disillusionment that followed, and the return to education with new purpose.

Why I write

Writing is how I process. Every blog post is an act of reflective practice — taking the messy, complicated work of education and technology and trying to make sense of it. Over twenty years, this practice has become inseparable from who I am as an educator. I write to learn, and I share so others can learn alongside me.
