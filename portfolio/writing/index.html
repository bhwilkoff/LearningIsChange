Why I write

Writing is how I process. Every blog post is an act of reflective practice — taking the messy, complicated work of education and technology and trying to make sense of it. Over twenty years, this practice has become inseparable from who I am as an educator. I write to learn, and I share so others can learn alongside me.

The Archive

Complete Archival Preservation

Every post since 2005 is preserved in full. The blog began at mrwilkoff.blogspot.com during the 2004–2005 school year, formally became Learning is Change in December 2007, and has been migrated from WordPress to a fully static GitHub Pages architecture while preserving every URL, every comment archive, and every piece of content. The full archive is searchable through the post database.
