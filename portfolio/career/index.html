Where I have worked

Career Timeline

I have been working in education since 2004, and every job on this list has been a different way of trying to make learning more human.

2026 – Present

Independent App Developer

Learning is Change: Archive Watch, Tidbits Trivia, Bsky Dreams, Boba Playbook

I design, build, and ship native apps as a one-person studio: four apps for phones, TVs, desktops, and the web, each from one shared core. Swift and SwiftUI, Kotlin and Compose, C# and Avalonia, BrightScript, and plain web. No servers, no paid tiers. Store submissions and content pipelines run on GitHub Actions. I wrote the method down as an open-source app template so that I do not have to solve the same problems twice. See the apps →

  • Native App Development
  • Cross-Platform Parity
  • Store Submission & Release
  • CI/CD & Device QA
  • AI-Assisted Engineering
2019 – Present

Associate Director of Admissions Operations

Minerva University

I lead admissions operations for a department that reads 25,000+ applications a year, with a team of 50+ in 7 hubs around the world. I built the data dashboards, the AI-assisted evaluation tools, and the playbooks the team runs on, including how we use Claude and Gemini in the work without losing the human judgment. I also train the team live in Minerva's Active Learning Platform and design the routines that hold a distributed team together across time zones.

  • Admissions Operations
  • Distributed Team Leadership
  • AI-Augmented Workflows
  • Data Dashboards
  • Global Education
2018 – 2019

Program Manager, Digital Education & Academic Technology

University of Colorado, School of Dental Medicine

Academic technology lead for the dental school. I designed teaching environments around VR, XR, and haptics, and I advised faculty on instructional design, digital media, and which new technologies were worth their time.

  • VR/XR & Haptics
  • Academic Technology
  • Health Sciences
  • Instructional Design
2013 – 2018

Director of Blended & Personalized Learning

Aurora Public Schools & Denver Public Schools

I led blended and personalized learning in two large urban districts, supporting 8,000+ teachers through coaching teams. I rolled out 1:1 iPad and Chromebook programs, built standards-based data systems for instruction, and designed a district-wide micro-credential program for technology skills.

  • Blended Learning
  • District Leadership
  • 1:1 Device Rollouts
  • Micro-credentialing
  • Instructional Data Systems
2013 – 2019

Vice President, Board of Directors

InnEdCO, Innovative Education Colorado

I ran the program side of InnEdCO, Colorado's largest edtech conference for teachers (about 1,000 of them a year). I built the Google Workspace databases and the app integrations behind 200+ sessions, the livestreams, and the conversation around them.

  • Board Governance
  • Conference Operations
  • Google Workspace
  • EdTech Community
2010 – 2011

Online Community & UX Manager

Edmodo

Edmodo had 85 million users when I was its 13th employee. I designed what became its Help & Training Center, hosted a webinar every week, and ran online communities of teachers from around the world. I learned a great deal there, and I stayed less than a year.

  • UX Design
  • Global Platform (85M users)
  • Community Building
  • Online Learning
2009 – 2010

Online Learning Specialist

Douglas County School District

Project manager and instructional designer for eDCSD, the district's K-12 online school. I moved its content across three learning management systems and ran Google Workspace training for the whole district.

  • Instructional Design
  • LMS Transitions
  • K-12 Online School
  • Project Management
2004 – 2009

7th & 8th Grade English Language Arts Teacher

Cresthill Middle School · Douglas County School District

Where it started. I taught 7th and 8th grade English Language Arts and put wikis, blogs, and podcasts in front of middle schoolers before that was a normal thing to do. I started this blog in 2005, and in 2007 Edutopia and Yahoo named me their “Totally Wired Teacher of the Year.”

  • Classroom Teaching
  • English/Language Arts
  • Edutopia Award 2007
  • Early Blogger

A thread that connects every role

In a middle school classroom or a university admissions office, the belief is the same: technology should serve people, and learning should get more accessible, more personal, and more human because of it. Each job was a new place to test that.
