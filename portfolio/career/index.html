Professional Journey

Career Timeline

Two decades of working at the intersection of education and technology, always with the goal of making learning more human.

2019 – Present

Associate Director of Admissions Operations

Minerva University

Lead global admissions operations for a department processing 25,000+ applications a year, managing a distributed team of 50+ across 7 international hubs. Built the custom data dashboards, AI-augmented evaluation tooling, and operations playbooks the team runs on — including context repositories and evaluation patterns for using Claude and Gemini in admissions workflows. Facilitate synchronous training in an Active Learning Platform and design rituals that scale across time zones.

  • Admissions Operations
  • Distributed Team Leadership
  • AI-Augmented Workflows
  • Data Dashboards
  • Global Education
2018 – 2019

Program Manager, Digital Education & Academic Technology

University of Colorado — School of Dental Medicine

Academic technology lead for the School of Dental Medicine. Designed teaching environments around emerging modalities — VR/XR and haptics — and provided strategic leadership on instructional design, digital media, and emerging technology adoption in health sciences education.

  • VR/XR & Haptics
  • Academic Technology
  • Health Sciences
  • Instructional Design
2013 – 2018

Director of Blended & Personalized Learning

Aurora Public Schools & Denver Public Schools

Led blended and personalized learning across two large urban districts, supporting 8,000+ teachers through coaching teams. Rolled out 1:1 iPad and Chromebook programs, built standards-based instructional data systems, and designed a district-wide digital micro-credentialing program for technology competencies.

  • Blended Learning
  • District Leadership
  • 1:1 Device Rollouts
  • Micro-credentialing
  • Instructional Data Systems
2013 – 2019

Vice President, Board of Directors

InnEdCO — Innovative Education Colorado

Led professional learning operations for InnEdCO, Colorado's largest EdTech educator conference (~1,000 educators annually). Built the Google Workspace databases and app integrations supporting 200+ concurrent sessions, livestreams, and the conference's social engagement layer.

  • Board Governance
  • Conference Operations
  • Google Workspace
  • EdTech Community

Online Community & UX Manager

Edmodo

For a global learning platform serving 85 million users, led the design project that became Edmodo's worldwide online Help & Training Center. Hosted weekly webinars and facilitated international online communities of educators, establishing a culture of collaborative pedagogy at platform scale.

  • UX Design
  • Global Platform (85M users)
  • Community Building
  • Online Learning
2009 – 2010

Online Learning Specialist

Douglas County School District

Lead project manager and instructional designer for eDCSD, the district's K-12 online school. Managed content and platform transitions across three different learning management systems and ran district-wide Google Workspace training.

  • Instructional Design

  • K-12 Online School
  • Project Management
2004 – 2009

7th & 8th Grade English Language Arts Teacher

Cresthill Middle School · Douglas County School District

Where it all started. Teaching 7th and 8th grade English Language Arts, integrating technology into the classroom in ways that were ahead of their time. 2005 – Present

Learning is Change

Personal Blog, Writing & Building Practice

Began blogging in 2005 — what became a two-decade practice of public reflection on education, technology, and what it means to learn. Over 2,000 posts, 400+ videos, and, more recently, a portfolio of shipped native apps. The daily discipline of creating and sharing that runs underneath every role above.

A thread that connects every role

Whether in a middle school classroom or a university admissions office, the constant has been a deep belief that technology should serve people — making learning more accessible, more personal, and more human. Every position has been a chance to put that belief into practice at a different scale.
