# The company I keep now…

[![](https://i0.wp.com/edublogawards.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/11/nommostinfpost.png?resize=298%2C194)](http://edublogawards.com/2007/most-influential-blog-post-2007/)

I’m not sure how many people truly mean it when they say it, but I am truly honored by my inclusion in the [Most Influential Blog Post](http://edublogawards.com/2007/most-influential-blog-post-2007/) category of the [2007 Edublog Awards](http://edublogawards.com/) for [The Ripe Environment.](http://yongesonne.edublogs.org/2007/06/29/the-ripe-environment/)

[To be along side Karl Fisch and his amazing work to create a mirror for our schools…](http://thefischbowl.blogspot.com/2007/09/is-it-okay-to-be-technologically.html)

[To be next to Konrad Glogowski and his visual portrayal of pedagogy…](http://www.teachandlearn.ca/blog/2007/10/27/how-to-grow-a-blog/)

[To be nestled in with Scott Mcleod and his viral networking power and ability to see the greatness in a single idea…](http://www.dangerouslyirrelevant.org/2007/01/gone_fischin.html)

[To be among Kris Bradburn and the challenges put out by Wandering Ink…](http://wanderingink.wordpress.com/2007/05/23/how-to-prevent-another-leonardo-da-vinci/)

That is what I am most honored by.

Thanks to all who nominated me. If you care to, please vote for this blog. I don’t imagine I have written as influential of blog posts as the above bloggers, but I would like it not to be an absolute slaughter in the polls.
