# 04.18.07

Cores 1-4:

- Guess how much of our Academy of Discovery budget was set aside for us at the meeting last night?

Core 1:

- Should leaders have more privileges than followers? Why or why not?
- Read chapter 4 in Animal Farm.  How does the farm change over time?
- Can any society keep the passion and ideal of the first few days/years?

- Work on your Utopias with a specific goal for your 15 minutes.

Core 2:

- How do you promote the societal good of justice in your everyday life?
- Work on your -Isms with a specific goal in mind.

Core 3:

- If you could, would you change the way life works so that you would:  Remain a child forever
- Be Born into adulthood
- Slow down or speed up the transition from childhood to adulthood

- Read the rest of the chapter:  What is gold?

Core 4:

- Check out the [front page!](http://www.wikispaces.com/)
- What is the safeguard on corruption in your utopia? Can anyone get around it?
- Work on your Utopia with specific goals in mind.
