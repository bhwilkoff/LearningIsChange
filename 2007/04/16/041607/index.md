# 04.16.07

Core 1:

- What is the easiest way to hide corruption within a society?
- Read Chapter 4 in Animal Farm.
- Work on utopias.  Who in your utopia is corruptible?
- Figure out your 7+ Commandments.

Core 2:

- If this were your metaphor for life, how would you describe it?
- Check-In with -Ism. How many projects do you have done (including audio essay)?
- Work on -Ism with specific goals.

Core 3:

-  What is the one thing that you have hidden from people, hoping that they would never find out about?
- Read about Ponyboy and Johnny’s transformation.  Can you still be a greaser without the grease?
- What are the essential parts of your identity?
