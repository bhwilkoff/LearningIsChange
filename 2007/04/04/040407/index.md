# 04.04.07

Core 1:

- How can we [discuss our worlds?](http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com/message/list/home)
- New examples of Utopian integration.
- Introduction to [the reaction novel.](http://odeo.com/channel/106499/view/)
- Read about and discuss the many levels of Animal Farm.

Core 2:

- What does [formal and informal discussion](http://discoveryisms.wikispaces.com/message/list/aznchicken17) look like on the wiki?
- Making Belief Statements real  [This I Believe](http://www.npr.org/templates/story/story.php?storyId=4538138)
- [Standpoint](http://www.standpoint.com/)

- Listen to This I Believe Essays and start to narrow down which belief you want to write into your Audio Essay.

Core 3:

- Write-on: What is the perfect group of friends? (What types of people do you have to have in the group so that it works?)
- Learn about the Greasers from the rest of chapter 1.
- Fill out the graphic organizer for the characters.

Core 4:

- What new ways of revealing your utopia are you trying? (or do you want to try?)
- Explore a few of the most recent additions.  [Olien](http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com/olein)

- [Dnalsi](http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com/Cody%27s+Utopia)

- Discuss measurable goals, and one more day of creation without rubric.
