# 04.13.07

Core 1:

- Creating interactivity on your wiki using [sPresent.](http://www.spresent.com/)
- Wiki work and troubleshooting.

Core 2:

-  How was your -Ism shaped by the following events:  9/11
- The first time you got the wind knocked out of you.
- Your first break-up (with a friend or boy/girlfriend).
- Your first fight with your parents.
- A time when you got mad at God or the universe.
- A time when you thought things were unfair.
- The first time you got made fun of.
- [Insert a specific event that you think changed your outlook on the world].

- Work on belief statement essays and sPresent interactive answers.

Core 3:

- How do you resolve the dilemma of a fight without resorting to violence?
- Read “The Good Part.”  When is self-defense not really self-defense?

Core 4:

- What will you do with your Utopia once it is created?
- Work on your Utopia with specific goals like:  Finishing a sPresent
- Finishing the Origins (or another category)
- Take a screenshot of your planet, flag, etc.
