# 04.17.07

Cores 1, 2, 4:

- Watch [Inquiring Minds Need to Know](http://academyofdiscovery.wikispaces.com/Inquiring%20Minds%20Want%20to%20Know) and introduce the Academy of Discovery:  What should we change before we go to the parent committee and ask for money?
- What questions would you have after our presentation?

- Work on your Blog or Wiki and submit a del.icio.us post for Sem2_Week13.

Core 3:

- Why is your physical identity so valuable to you?
- Do you think that one decision should affect you for the rest of your life?
- Do you have an anti-hero that you look up to, like Johnny does to Dally?
- What would you have picked up from the grocery store if you knew that you might have to hide out for a long time and you didn’t bring anything with you?
- What would being a fugitive from the law be like?
