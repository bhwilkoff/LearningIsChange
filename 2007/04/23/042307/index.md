# 04.23.07

Core 1:

- Check out the [Clustr Map.](http://www2.clustrmaps.com/counter/maps.php?url=http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com)  How does this change the nature of our writing/project?

- Discuss-On: What kind of leader usually wins a fight/debate?
- Read the Snowball and Napoleon showdown.  How does this fundamentally change the society?
- Is it better or worse than having your two leaders argue all of the time?

- Work on your Utopias with specific goals in mind.

Core 2:

- Social Movement Theory #1: [All movements have an initiating event.](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_movements#Dynamics_of_social_movements)  What is, or will be, the initiating event of your -Ism that will allow people to rally around it?

- Work on your -Isms and work toward a movement.

Core 3:

- Introduce [My Meaning.](http://mymeaning.wikispaces.com/)  Why [a wiki?](http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com/)
- Why My Meaning?

- Why would Cherry spy for the greasers after a greaser had just killed her boyfriend?
- Take a look at what happens next.

- Check out the [Clustr Map.](http://www2.clustrmaps.com/counter/maps.php?url=http://discoveryutopias.wikispaces.com)  How does this change the nature of our writing/project?

- Work on your Utopias with specific goals in mind.
