# 09.21.07

Core 1:

- Write-on: How can the specific words that we use affect how persuasive we are in our writing or speaking?
- Continue working on [“Tom Sawyer Whitewashing the Fence.”](http://www.pbs.org/marktwain/learnmore/writings_tom.html)  Does Tom make fallacious arguments or real ones?
- How does he convince others to do his work for him?

- Extensions:  Plan out how you plan to convince your parents/brother/sister to do something that you don’t want to do without the use of fallacious arguments. Write out how you have accomplished your goal in your blog.

Core 2:

- Write-on: Create three short sentences about something sitting on your desk or in your pockets.
- Read The Pen Commandments and take a look at the Pacemaker Syndrome, Conjunctionitis, and the Case Studies.
- Extensions:  For next Friday, brainstorm what your case study would be about.

Core 3:

- Finish presenting the 1940’s Smart Notebook Files.
- Listen to and watch the speeches by FDR and fill out the [Speech Analysis Form](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/space/showimage/speechform.JPG).  [Address at Hyde Park, New York.](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/mediaplay.php?id=16139&admin=32)
- [Statement on Signing the Social Security Act.](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/mediaplay.php?id=14916&admin=32)

- Compare and contrast [FDR’s speech after the Pearl Harbor attacks](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/mediaplay.php?id=16053&admin=32) with [George W. Bush’s Speech after the 9/11 attacks](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/ws/index.php?pid=58057&st=&st1=).
- Discuss the speeches in terms of change, tradition, and sacrificing individual needs for the needs of many.
- Extensions:  Finish the Speech Analysis Form.
