# 02.08.07

Core 1:

- Write-on: If you had a machine that could create anything, would you use it? Why or why not?
- Read: [How the World Was Saved.](http://www.lem.pl/cyberiadinfo/english/opowiadania/opowiadania7.htm)
- What is the virtue of thinking through each of your actions?

Core 2:

- Write-on: Finish this quotation any way that you think makes sense and explain your thinking:  A government is best when it governs…

- Read “Civil Disobedience.” and discuss the role of government in a belief structure.

Core 3:

- Discuss-On: What makes a story good?
- Read about [storyboarding.](http://www.exposure.co.uk/eejit/storybd/)
- Practice the [concept of storyboarding.](http://www.plugincinema.com/plugin/film_school/interactive.html)
- Researching your topic so that you can develop your storyboard.

Core 4:

- Groups 1 and 2 present their Survival Societies.
