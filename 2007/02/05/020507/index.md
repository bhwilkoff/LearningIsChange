# 02.05.07

Core 1:

- Do your Write-ons on the [Discovery Message Board.](http://z6.invisionfree.com/Discovery_Discussion/)
- Flip back to the Vocabulary books with some Vocab Basketball?

Core 2:

- Write-on: What is the most significant movement of our time (literary, social, political, etc.)? How was it defined/started?
- Explore the definitions of Transcendentalism through [author’s quotations.](http://www.vcu.edu/engweb/transcendentalism/ideas/tr-aldef.html)

Core 3:

- What are the requirements for our digital stories that you should be graded upon?
- Point of View
-        A Dramatic Question
-        Emotional Content
-        The Gift of your Voice
-        The Power of the Soundtrack
-        Economy
- Pacing

- Jump back into vocabulary with some Vocab Basketball.

Core 4:

- Write-on: When survival is the most pressing part of your life, does life itself take on a rhythm, [a music](http://books.google.com/books?id=kArzYZhf6lIC&pg=RA1-PA1&lpg=RA1-PA1&vq=music&dq=the+pearl+john+steinbeck&psp=1&sig=LzyPLKaapQBp5E2ZAR81OQ6iOg0)?
- Talk about breaking one another’s societies with plausable What-If statements.
- Work on your Survival Simulations.
