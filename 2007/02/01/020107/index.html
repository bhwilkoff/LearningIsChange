Core 1:

  1. Check out the rest of the “Ideal” vacations, meals, and cars.
  2. What does your ideal item say about the society that you think is perfect?

  3. What is a utopia and what is a dystopia?

  1. Write-On: Describe the connection you feel between yourself and a good friend or family member.
  2. Read the “Noiseless Patient Spider”
  3. What is your gossamer thread?

  1. Establish Cell Phone rules for class.
  2. Preview digital storytelling resources:
