# 05.22.07

Core 1:

- Watch Animal Farm and look for how the animals’ acceptance of napoleon’s leadership changes from novel to movie.
- Discuss your Utopia with Mr. Wilkoff.

Core 2:

- Continue discussion of how advertising stereotypes influence your beliefs.
- Watch [The Persuaders](http://www.pbs.org/wgbh/pages/frontline/shows/persuaders/view/) and discuss just how much like advertising a movement can truly be.
- Discuss -Ism with Mr. Wilkoff

Core 3:

- Watch The Outsiders and find how your meaning changes when put into a more visual form.  How do the skipped parts add to or take away from the meaning of the movie.

- Discuss your Meaning with Mr. Wilkoff

Core 4:

- Watch “Animal Farm” and discuss how your utopia will or will not become corrupt like theirs.
- Discuss your Utopia with Mr. Wilkoff
