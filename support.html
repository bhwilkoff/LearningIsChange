Getting a fast, useful answer

A support request tells me the most when it includes a little context. The buttons above pre-fill a template — just fill in the blanks.

1 · Where

Which app & platform

iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, Android, or the web — and the app version if you can find it in Settings or About.

2 · What

What happened

What you expected, what actually happened, and the steps to reproduce it. A screenshot or screen recording is gold.

3 · When

How often

Every time, or just once? Right after an update? Knowing the pattern usually points straight at the cause.

Something else?

General questions, feedback, and everything not listed

Feature ideas, partnership questions, press, or an app not in the list — it all comes to the same place, and it all reaches me directly. I read every message.

Email support

Prefer not to use email links? Show the address and copy it manually. The address is loaded by your browser rather than printed in the page, which keeps it out of the hands of spam bots that scrape the raw HTML.
