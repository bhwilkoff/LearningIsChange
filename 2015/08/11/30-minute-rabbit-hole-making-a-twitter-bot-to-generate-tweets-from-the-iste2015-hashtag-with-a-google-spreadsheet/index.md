# 30 Minute Rabbit Hole: Making a Twitter Bot to generate tweets from the #ISTE2015 hashtag with a Google Spreadsheet

[![Ocaptain](https://i0.wp.com/104.155.150.224/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/Ocaptain-1024x6571.jpg?resize=687%2C441)](https://i0.wp.com/104.155.150.224/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/Ocaptain.jpg)

So, I found this amazing tutorial for creating a TwitterBot using Google Spreadsheet. I wanted to put it to good use, so I made a Twitter Bot that leverages the archive of over 117,000 tweets from the #ISTE2015 hashtag. If you are so inclined to see the new insights that are generated from this wealth of knowledge, please feel free to follow [@obotmybot](https://twitter.com/obotmybot).

> Introducing the newly upgraded SSBot: A tool for making a Twitter bot with Google Spreadsheets. Version 0.4 fixes some major bugs and adds support for additional bot types.
