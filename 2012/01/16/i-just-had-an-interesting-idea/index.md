# I just had an interesting idea…

I just had an interesting idea…

Imagine an application on your computer that could take any image file and “draw” it. What I mean is that there should be a program that would sketch out different elements of that image until it came into view.

My thought is that something like this already exists, but I’m not sure exactly what you would call it.

Any thoughts on the right direction to search in?
