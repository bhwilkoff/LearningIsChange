I bought the Google+ Blog WordPress plugin from you and I love it. I did have one question, though:

Is there any way for me allow the plugin to post to a custom post type rather than just as a “post”? If it requires some editing of the plugin, that is okay. Just point me in the right direction. 

Again, thanks for all of your hard work on this. It is awesome.
