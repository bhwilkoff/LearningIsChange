# #OpenInnovation and #CoCreation

I have been researching two terms that are heavily used in the development and innovation communities to speak about their processes and ways in which to include the user in development: Open Innovation and Co-Creation. These terms have been around for a little while, but they are more recently being taken seriously by small and large organizations. Anyway, I thought I would share the research in case you should want to take a look as well. I think that both of these terms and ideas can help to influence our own processes, no matter what they may be. It is a pretty extensive reading list, so please don’t feel like you have to read it all!
