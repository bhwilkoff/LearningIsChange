# 10.11.06

Core 1:

- Play vocabulary basketball to review for unit 7 quiz.
- Take unit 7 quiz.
- Do [partnered good parts](http://www.thinkfree.com/filelink.tfo?filemasterno=466308&filekey=eqxn3jg2nv) to fulfill quarter 1 requirement.

Core 2:

- Discuss-On: How did you tackle the sesquipedalian paragraph? What is your thought process when you encounter a seemingly impenetrable paragraph?
- Take a second look at the paragraph.
- Practice and act out a few sesquipedalian theater pieces.
- Do [partnered good parts](http://www.thinkfree.com/filelink.tfo?filemasterno=466308&filekey=eqxn3jg2nv) to fulfill quarter 1 requirement.

Core 3:

- Introduce [tickets for The Trip To Essay Land.](http://www.thinkfree.com/filelink.tfo?filemasterno=340569&filekey=05z0rnbre0)
- Practice writing thesis statements.
- Do [partnered good parts](http://www.thinkfree.com/filelink.tfo?filemasterno=466308&filekey=eqxn3jg2nv) to fulfill quarter 1 requirement.

Core 4:

- Collect Martin Luther King Speech Analysis.
- How did you attack reading [The Declaration](http://www.duke.edu/eng169s2/group1/lex3/hyprdecl.htm)? What is your thought process when you encounter a seemingly impenetrable paragraph?
- Discuss the reasons for changes in the draft.
- Why was this document so successful? What in the language makes it so persuasive? (Word Choice, Figurative Language, Allusion, Sentence Structure, Concrete and abstract images.
- Do [partnered good parts](http://www.thinkfree.com/filelink.tfo?filemasterno=466308&filekey=eqxn3jg2nv) to fulfill quarter 1 requirement.
