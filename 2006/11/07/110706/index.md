# 11.07.06

Cores 1 and 2:

- [Randomly select username for celebrity commenting.](http://dotnet.borland.com/random/client.aspx)
- Write-On: Comment on one post from the “Celebrity User.” [You may use a comment starter](http://adavis.pbwiki.com/Significant%20Comments) to help you make a more authentic comment.
- Write your weekly authentic for this week and don’t forget to nominate for [The Authenticity Awards.](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/The+Authenticity+Awards)

Core 3:

- What is “wrong” with the following sentences:  Harry Potter is a great character.
- I love chocolate.
- The creatures in The Lord of The Rings movies are scary.

- Discuss [lost vs. found](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/Trip+to+Essay+Land#Day%207) sentences.
- Talk about levels of detail in body/supporting paragraphs through [Mountain Outlining.](http://www.thinkfree.com/filelink.tfo?filemasterno=329193&filekey=3bz4l5q6qo)
- Write a body paragraph for your bloggged essay.

Core 4:

- [Randomly select username for celebrity commenting.](http://dotnet.borland.com/random/client.aspx)
- Discuss the important points of the [Football as Blogging Article.](http://coolcatteacher.blogspot.com/2006/11/blogging-is-like-football-what.html)
- Write your weekly authentic for this week and don’t forget to nominate for [The Authenticity Awards.](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/The+Authenticity+Awards)
