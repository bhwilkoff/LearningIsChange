# 11.21.06

Cores 1-4:

- Discuss rules for guest blogging (something simple enough that even a parent could understand them).

- Develop proper usage for video in your blogs.  What about [web junk](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_Junk_20)?
- The concept of building.
- The concept of school appropriate.

- Nominate, Comment, Finish up and drafts before the holiday.
