# Not Tagged – The Book Meme

I was not tagged to contribute to the book meme, but that is okay because I mostly thought that memes were a little bit hokey. That is until I started looking into memology. I am really enamored with the concept of viral ideas, that we contribute to one anothers conciousness in such a contagious way. I also think that is a great idea to talk about reading as having a large impact our lives. So, here is my contribution to the book meme, even though no one asked for it.

1. One book that changed your life:
[![Slaughterhouse-Five](https://i0.wp.com/images.amazon.com/images/P/0440180295.01._BO2%2C204%2C203%2C200_PIsitb-dp-500-arrow%2CTopRight%2C45%2C-64_AA240_SH20_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/reader/0440180295/ref=sib_dp_pt/102-1245759-9041750#reader-link)Slaughter-House Five. I will always remember the first time I realized the true power of “So it goes.” After every death in the book (including the deaths of inanimate objects) this phrase is echoed. It makes me so happy that world goes on after us, that everything around us is normal, even death. Of course, in the book, time travel and aliens are normal, so maybe I keep missing the point.

2. One book that you’ve read more than once:

[![The Catcher in the Rye](https://i0.wp.com/ec1.images-amazon.com/images/P/0316769487.01._AA240_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/images/0316769487/ref=dp_image_0/102-1245759-9041750?ie=UTF8&n=283155&s=books)Catcher in the Rye. Every time I read this book I am at a new stage of my life. I identify with a different part of Holden. I used to understand his rebellion so much better because I was rebellious. Now I understand his preservation of innocence because I want that for my daughter.

3. One book you’d want on a desert island:
[![The Old Man and The Sea](https://i0.wp.com/ec1.images-amazon.com/images/P/0684801221.01._AA187_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/images/0684801221/ref=dp_image_0/102-1245759-9041750?ie=UTF8&n=283155&s=books)The Old Man and the Sea. I think if I were on a desert island I would finally have enough time to analyze the meaning of all of the simple and beautiful words.

4. One book that made you laugh:
[![A Heartbreaking Work of Staggering Genius (Vintage)](https://i0.wp.com/ec3.images-amazon.com/images/P/0375725784.01._BO2%2C204%2C203%2C200_PIsitb-dp-500-arrow%2CTopRight%2C45%2C-64_AA240_SH20_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/reader/0375725784/ref=sib_dp_pt/102-1245759-9041750#reader-link)A Heartbreaking Work of Stagering Genius. Dave Eggar’s humor is some of the ripest and most intelligent I have known. I can’t help but laugh at his tragedy.

5. One book that made you cry:
[![A Heartbreaking Work of Staggering Genius (Vintage)](https://i0.wp.com/ec3.images-amazon.com/images/P/0375725784.01._BO2%2C204%2C203%2C200_PIsitb-dp-500-arrow%2CTopRight%2C45%2C-64_AA240_SH20_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/reader/0375725784/ref=sib_dp_pt/102-1245759-9041750#reader-link)A Heartbreaking Work of Staggering Genius. He lost his mother and father within a few months of one another. The weight of loss in his story is so overwhelming that it is hard to imagine reading it without at least welling up once.

6. One book that you wish had been written:
Simple Answers Suck: A book about the inherent complexity of searching for anything worth knowing.

7. One book that you wish had never been written:
[![Toxin](https://i0.wp.com/ec1.images-amazon.com/images/P/0425166619.01._BO2%2C204%2C203%2C200_PIsitb-dp-500-arrow%2CTopRight%2C45%2C-64_AA240_SH20_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/reader/0425166619/ref=sib_dp_pt/102-1245759-9041750#reader-link)Toxin. I had to teach this trash novel once in a Modern Literature course. It has the worst dialogue imaginable and the characters could not be more flat. I eventually had to teach it as an example of bad writing.

8. One book you’re currently reading:
[![The Know-It-All: One Man](https://i0.wp.com/images.amazon.com/images/P/0743250621.01._BO2%2C204%2C203%2C200_PIsitb-dp-500-arrow%2CTopRight%2C45%2C-64_AA240_SH20_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/reader/0743250621/ref=sib_dp_pt/102-1245759-9041750#reader-link)Know-It-All. I have great respect for anyone who reads the entire Encyclopaedia Britannica and doesn’t break up his marraige in the process.

9. One book you’ve been meaning to read:
[![A Novel](https://i0.wp.com/ec1.images-amazon.com/images/P/0060529709.01._BO2%2C204%2C203%2C200_PIsitb-dp-500-arrow%2CTopRight%2C45%2C-64_AA240_SH20_SCLZZZZZZZ_.jpg?w=1225)](http://www.amazon.com/gp/reader/0060529709/ref=sib_dp_pt/102-1245759-9041750#reader-link)Everything is Illuminated. This is a great movie and I have heard that the book is even better. I can’t wait to get some time to dive into it.
