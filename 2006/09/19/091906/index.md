- Re-Introduce [the concept of a Building Comment](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/better+blogging#build) and [show example](http://bhwilkoff.edublogs.org/2006/09/11/building-comment-1/).

- Afix your signature to the [Discovery Blogging Rules for 2006-2007](http://www.writely.com/View.aspx?docid=dcjpnst3_23gnpshf).
- Go down to the library to work on [Nominating](http://www.jotform.com/form/62474215164), [Commenting](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/better+blogging) (Building or regular), or writing your third [Weekly Authentic](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/The+Weekly+Authentic).
