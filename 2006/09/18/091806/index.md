# 09.18.06

Cores 1-4:

- Final Review of [Blogging Rules](http://www.writely.com/View.aspx?docid=dcjpnst3_23gnpshf).
- [Psycodude’s Apology Letter](http://bhwilkoff.edublogs.org/2006/09/18/psycodudes-apology/).
- [September 11th Memorial](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/911+memorial).

Core 1:

- Check “Synonyms and Antonyms” for Unit 7 in vocabulary book.
- Collect any “Word Stories” not turned in on Friday.
- Group Write-On: How many different types of writing are there?
- Group Discussion questions:

- How are they different?
- How do you have to approach them differently?
- Why would someone choose to write in one genre rather than another one?
- Why are some more popular than others?
- Are there any more important than others?
- Which are you most interested in? Why?

- Do a few [Good Parts](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/The+Good+Parts). Core 2:

- Write-On: Which words are indicative of change? Which words are indicative of tradition?
- Listen to and watch the speeches by FDR and fill out the [Speech Analysis Form](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/space/showimage/speechform.JPG).
- [Address at Hyde Park, New York.](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/mediaplay.php?id=16139&admin=32)
- [Statement on Signing the Social Security Act.](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/mediaplay.php?id=14916&admin=32)

- Compare and contrast [FDR’s speech after the Pearl Harbor attacks](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/mediaplay.php?id=16053&admin=32) with [George W. Bush’s Speech after the 9/11 attacks](http://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/ws/index.php?pid=58057&st=&st1=).
- Discuss the speeches in terms of change and tradition.

Core 3:

- Check “Synonyms and Antonyms” for Unit 1 in vocabulary book.
- Collect any “Word Stories” not turned in on Friday.
- Write-On: What is an essay and why would anyone want to write one?
- Group Brainstorm: What is writing an essay like? What would you compare it to? (A walk in the park? Beating a dead horse? Painting a picture?
- Introduce [my metaphor](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/Trip+to+Essay+Land) for an essay.

Core 4:

- Write-On: What is social change? What kind of social change do you believe is good and bad?
- Presentation of Change by self-selected group.
- How do our truths of change inform our understanding of social change?
- How do books, writing, and words influence social change.
- Do a few [Good Parts](http://discovery0607.wikispaces.com/The+Good+Parts).
