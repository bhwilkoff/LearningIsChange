If you are implementing Blended Learning as a Principal, Teacher, or Coach, please RSVP for this event next week.

November 8, 2013 86 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

If you are implementing Blended Learning as a Principal, Teacher, or Coach, please RSVP for this event next week. The DPS Blended Learning affinity group will be hosting a Roundtable via a Hangout on Air. We would like to have at least a 6 live participants that the principals in this group will be able to ask questions and discuss ideas with.

I will send a direct link for the Hangout if you RSVP early enough. Looking forward to learning from you!
