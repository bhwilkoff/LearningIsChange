# The new google drive app for iOS now comes with multiple account support.

The new google drive app for iOS now comes with multiple account support. I think this could be huge for iPad schools. This would mean that multiple kids could use an iPad and switch accounts easily, thus making the iPad a multi-user device (for this purpose anyway).
