I’m really excited about getting things started back up as a regular part of my practice with The Fellowship. I’ve realized just how important this group is to helping me figure out things that I want to focus on. But, before I start to lead in one direction or another, I would love to ask all of you: What do you want this group to be about this year and how do you want to expand our reach to include more participants?
