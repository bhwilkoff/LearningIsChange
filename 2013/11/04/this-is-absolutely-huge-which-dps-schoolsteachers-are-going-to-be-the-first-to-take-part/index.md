# This is absolutely huge! Which DPS schools/teachers are going to be the first to take part?

This is absolutely huge! Which DPS schools/teachers are going to be the first to take part?

Originally shared by Google

Today we’re launching #ConnectedClassrooms, a new program that enables students around the world to take “virtual field trips” through Google+ Hangouts to places they’d otherwise never be able to explore. We’re kicking things off today with field trips to the Seattle Aquarium, the Minnesota Zoo and the Solar Impulse hangar, with 20+ other partners hosting virtual field trips soon. If you’re a K-12 teacher interested in taking your classroom on a #VirtualFieldTrip, learn more and sign up at [g.co/connectedclassrooms](http://g.co/connectedclassrooms).
