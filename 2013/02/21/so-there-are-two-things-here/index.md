# So, there are two things here:

So, there are two things here:

1. This video collaboration between students from around the world is amazing. It is completely homegrown (read: no formal teacher support), and I love what they have created.

2. I want my own Fellowship. I would love to have 6 other people that would be game for creating videos on a single channel and respond to one another in a weekly (or semi-weekly) cycle (i.e., we would all pick one day of the week).

I’m game for anyone taking part, but I would love to see what you think of the idea. I will likely be having more conversations about this with new folks, but I’ll tag a few to see if the conversation here starts the ball rolling.  Gallit Zvi, Sheri Edwards, Darren Kuropatwa Rodd Lucier
