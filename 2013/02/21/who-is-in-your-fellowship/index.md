# Who is in your Fellowship?

I came across the singe most amazing student collaboration I have ever seen. While that may sound like hyperbole, it isn’t. I love the fact that 7 "strangers" from around the world came together and for two years produced video blogs for one another about topics they loved. While they are stopping their collaboration at the end of February, I encourage you to check it out because there are hundreds of videos to explore.

Also, I am looking to start a fellowship of my own… so, let me know if you want in.
