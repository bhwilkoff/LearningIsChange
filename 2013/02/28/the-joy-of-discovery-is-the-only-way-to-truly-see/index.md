# The Joy of Discovery is the Only Way To Truly See

Sometimes we forget about the joy of discovery in favor of all of our other responsibilities. Joelle Warsh called attention to this, and I couldn’t stop thinking about it.  I believe that we engage in the process of Seeing things clearly by experiencing that process of discovery. How do you see that process of Discovery?
