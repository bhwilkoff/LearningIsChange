Second-Chance Serendipity and Your Long Tail Learning

February 26, 2013 78 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

I was flabbergasted to learn that one of my favorite current collaborators and I had shared a conversation four years ago. Neither of us recognized the opportunity that serendipity was presenting to us, and yet we got a second chance to learn from one another. Because we both have our own "Long Tail Learning", we were able to recognize our mutual interest and capitalize on it.
