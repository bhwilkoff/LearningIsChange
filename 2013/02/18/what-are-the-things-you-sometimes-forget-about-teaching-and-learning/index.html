  16. Gallit ZviMarch 3, 2013

    Sometimes I forget that not everybody is on the same journey as I am…and that is what makes this all so interesting…and so I need to find a way to connect with them in a way that is meaningful to them and their journey. 

    Sometimes I forget that learning isn’t the only important thing going on in my classroom (but I am reminded when I get a new student and realize I need to go shopping to buy her some clothes), but building relationships, a sense of community and caring for one and other is so important too!

    Sometimes I forget how many amazing educators there are out there and how fortunate I am to have them in my PLN.

    And sometimes I spend so much time on twitter and forget about google+ and am late to the party for these types of things…so sorry…thank you for giving me the opportunity to reflect…
