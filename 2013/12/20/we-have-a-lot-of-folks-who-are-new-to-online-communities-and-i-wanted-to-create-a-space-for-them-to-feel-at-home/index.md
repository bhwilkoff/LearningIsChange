# We have a lot of folks who are new to online communities, and I wanted to create a space for them to feel at home…

We have a lot of folks who are new to online communities, and I wanted to create a space for them to feel at home asking questions (whether technical or otherwise) and getting support.

So, if you are a newbie or even if you just have a question you don’t know who to ask, go ahead and join up so that we can start the conversation.
