So, the first prototype of my action plan (which I still have to actually write up) is ready for testing.

August 19, 2013 117 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

So, the first prototype of my action plan (which I still have to actually write up) is ready for testing. 

This form creates a Google Document “PD Plan” based upon your experience with Google Apps and how you fill out this form. This means that based upon your answers, you will get different tasks/resources in the Google Doc. Let me know what you think or if you have any ideas on making the concept better.

(Also, if anyone can figure out how to delay an “on form submit” trigger for a few seconds until the links are calculated, that would be great. Otherwise, the last person doesn’t receive their Google Doc because it doesn’t trigger correctly. )
