If your opinion, how is an online community of practice different than one done face to face?

August 12, 2013 34 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

If your opinion, how is an online community of practice different than one done face to face?

What can you do in a space like this that you cannot do in a physical setting?

6 comments archived from the original blog; comments are closed

  1. Jessica RaleighAugust 17, 2013

    An online community allows immediate, natural differentiation and choice because the learner can drive the pace and seek out content that is most relevant to them at any given moment. Connecting with like-minded individuals outside a physical space means learning from a variety of perspectives and encountering possibilities you may have not thought of in your own setting. The asynchronous, online environment gives every learner the chance to process others’ ideas and share thoughts in their own time. Taking the conversation outside the walls of a room provides more opportunities to meet the needs of every learner and ensure their engagement and success.
