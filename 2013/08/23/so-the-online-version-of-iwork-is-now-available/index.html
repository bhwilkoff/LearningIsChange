So, the online version of iWork is now available.

August 23, 2013 53 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

So, the online version of iWork is now available. This gives the ability to create documents, spreadsheets and presentations from the web with a really high fidelity for formatting and syncing with devices like the iPad.

I encourage you to check it out, especially if you have iPads in your classroom.
