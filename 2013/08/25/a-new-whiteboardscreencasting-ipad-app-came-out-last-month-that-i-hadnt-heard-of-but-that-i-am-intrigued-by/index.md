# A new whiteboard/screencasting iPad app came out last month that I hadn’t heard of, but that I am intrigued by.

A new whiteboard/screencasting iPad app came out last month that I hadn’t heard of, but that I am intrigued by.

The difference between this one and the others I have seen (explain everything), is that you can record from the camera while you are recording the screen as well. This would allow you to put more of “yourself” as the teacher into the videos you create, or more of the “students as teachers” into their own creations.

Also, the app is free, which is nice for trying it out.
