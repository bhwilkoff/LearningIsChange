Since this community helped to start this idea. Here it is, kinda sorta being realized.

Originally shared by Ben Wilkoff

So, here is the idea.

Let’s do some reflective practice vlogging in 2013. Not every day and not to the point of exhaustion. But, let’s create a community of people who want to collaborate through video.

Here is the kickoff blog post as well: /blog/2013/01/06/a-request-for-collaboration-reflective-practice-vlogging/

Come and join us, even if you aren’t quite sure about Vlogging. We aren’t sure either.
