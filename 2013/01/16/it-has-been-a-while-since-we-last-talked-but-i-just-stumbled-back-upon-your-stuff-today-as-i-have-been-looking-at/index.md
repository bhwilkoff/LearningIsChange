# It has been a while since we last talked, but I just stumbled back upon your stuff today as I have been looking at…

It has been a while since we last talked, but I just stumbled back upon your stuff today as I have been looking at educators who are interested in Vlogging. I just started a project with a bunch of folks to do just that in 2013, and I can’t believe that you are doing something so similar with your time this year as well. (Actually, I can believe it because that is the way ideas tend to work…)

I would love to have you post your vlogs into this community as you make them. I will be responding to your Fidgital video today as a part of my reflective practice, so thank you for making it! Anyway, keep doing what you are doing. I love it.
