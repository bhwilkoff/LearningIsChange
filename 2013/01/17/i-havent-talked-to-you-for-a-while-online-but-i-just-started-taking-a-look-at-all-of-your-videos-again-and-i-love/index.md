# I haven’t talked to you for a while online, but I just started taking a look at all of your videos again and I love…

I haven’t talked to you for a while online, but I just started taking a look at all of your videos again and I love the direction you have maintained and just how inclusive the communities you foster are. Thanks for subscribing to my youtube channel as well, as that is one of the reasons why I started looking back at some of the stuff you were working on.

I am creating a project during 2013 on doing Reflective Practice Vlogs for as many folks as want to participate this year. I would be honored to have you as a participant (someone who records videos, reacts to videos, or just lurks in the shadows). Let me know if you have any questions. I’m just excited about “seeing” you more often.
