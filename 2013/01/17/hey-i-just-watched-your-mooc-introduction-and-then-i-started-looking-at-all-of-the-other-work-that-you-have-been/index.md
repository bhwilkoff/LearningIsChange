# Hey, I just watched your MOOC introduction and then I started looking at all of the other work that you have been…

Hey, I just watched your MOOC introduction and then I started looking at all of the other work that you have been creating. I was pretty excited by the level of reflection going on in your work.

It is also why I thought you might be interested in a project that I am working on. I am cultivating a community of folks who are interested in doing reflective practice vlogging in 2013. We are recording videos of our learning and then reacting  and collaborating with one another through more videos. Let me know if you have any questions about it, but regardless I’m excited to keep up with your exploits on your youtube channel. Talk to you soon.
