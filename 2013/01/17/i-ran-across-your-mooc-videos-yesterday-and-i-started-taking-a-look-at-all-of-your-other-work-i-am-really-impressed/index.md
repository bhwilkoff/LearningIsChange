# I ran across your MOOC videos yesterday and I started taking a look at all of your other work. I am really impressed…

I ran across your MOOC videos yesterday and I started taking a look at all of your other work. I am really impressed with how you come at it and it made me think that you might be interested in a project that I am working on in 2013.

I am cultivating a community for teachers and learners to do reflective practice vlogging, collaborating and reacting through youtube videos. I’d be honored to have you join the group and poke around a bit (even if you don’t want to record a video just yet). Anyway, thanks for creating a lot of great things for me to dive into.
