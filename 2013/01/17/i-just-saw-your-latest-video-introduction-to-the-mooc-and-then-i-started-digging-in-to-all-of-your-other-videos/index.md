# I just saw your latest video introduction to the MOOC, and then I started digging in to all of your other videos.

I just saw your latest video introduction to the MOOC, and then I started digging in to all of your other videos. I really love what you have put together. It made me think that you might be interested in a project I am working on to create a community for reflective practice vlogging.

We are recording videos that reflect upon our learning (and others’ learning) and then we are reacting to them and collaborating on top of them through more videos. If that sounds like something you would be interested in, join this community. Also, let me know if you have any questions about it. Either way, keep doing what you are doing!
