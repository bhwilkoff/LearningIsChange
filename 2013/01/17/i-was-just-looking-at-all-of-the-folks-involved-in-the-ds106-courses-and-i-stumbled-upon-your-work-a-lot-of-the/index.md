# I was just looking at all of the folks involved in the ds106 courses, and I stumbled upon your work. A lot of the…

I was just looking at all of the folks involved in the ds106 courses, and I stumbled upon your work. A lot of the reflective stuff you have done really makes me think that you might be interested in a project I’m working on to do with Reflective Practice Vlogging.

Over the course of 2013, a small community of educators and I are recording reflective practice vlogs and then reacting to one another. I wonder if you might be interested in taking part (as a lurker, commenter, or video maker). Let me know if you have questions. Talk to you soon.
