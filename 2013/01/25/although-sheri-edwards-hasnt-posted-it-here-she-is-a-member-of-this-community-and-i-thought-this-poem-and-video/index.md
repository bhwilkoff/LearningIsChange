# Although Sheri Edwards hasn’t posted it here, she is a member of this community and I thought this poem and video…

Although Sheri Edwards hasn’t posted it here, she is a member of this community and I thought this poem and video were beautifully reflective:

Each of us: A Hub

Worlds within worlds

Family

Professional

Local –> Global

Each of us: A Hub

Reaching out

Gathering in

Making sense

Self –> Worlds

Each of us: A Hub

Spiraling

Connecting

Interacting

Worlds –> Worlds

Each and Together: Hubs

Communicating

Considering

Contributing

Remix –> Create

Each and Together: Hubs

Purpose and Action

Fluid Reactions

Worlds

Learning –> Change

Each and Together: Hubs

Linger here

Stay there

Learn and Act

Worlds –> Renewed
