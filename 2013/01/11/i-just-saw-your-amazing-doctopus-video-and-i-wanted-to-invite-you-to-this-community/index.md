# I just saw your amazing Doctopus video and I wanted to invite you to this community.

I just saw your amazing Doctopus video and I wanted to invite you to this community. We are interested in creating a group of youtubers that engage in reflective practice vlogging. We are currently responding to one another through video and having quite a bit of fun talking about our own learning environments. Let me know if you have any questions about the project, but I hope you check it out.
