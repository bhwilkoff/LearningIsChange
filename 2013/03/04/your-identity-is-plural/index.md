# Your Identity is Plural

I love the digital literacy conversation going on within the #etmooc, but I worry that the conversation around a digital identity is too singular in nature. We should be striving to make a plural identity, one that can change over time. And, we should strive to instill this concept of a plural identity into our learners from a young age.
