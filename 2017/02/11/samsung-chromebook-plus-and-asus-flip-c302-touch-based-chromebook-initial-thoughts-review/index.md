# Samsung Chromebook Plus and Asus Flip C302 Touch-based Chromebook Initial Thoughts Review

In this quick review of these two new chromebooks, I take a look at what it is like to have access to the Google Play store within a Chromebook environment. I talk about some of the opportunities as well as some of the drawbacks of working with these devices before ChromeOS and Android have fully gotten used to one another.
