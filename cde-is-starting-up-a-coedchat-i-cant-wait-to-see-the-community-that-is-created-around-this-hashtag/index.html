CDE is starting up a #COedchat. I can't wait to see the community that is created around this hashtag.

Updated February 19, 2014

I just saw on twitter that the Colorado Department of Education is launching a twitter chat once a month. I’m intrigued by the possibility and I would like to see as many people take part as possible. If you are in the mood, please check out their page and start posting questions and ideas on the hashtag. Let’s show the power of the Colorado twitterverse.

Monthly Colorado Education Twitter Chat (#COedchat) | CDE

Join us for a discussion on Twitter! Each month, the Colorado Department of Education and the Colorado Legacy Foundation hosts the #COedchat with experts on various education topics. This is your opportunity as an educator, parent, student or citizen to interact with experts on a specific topic and ask any questions you may have. Join the chat on the fourth Monday of every month from 4-4:45 p.m. Just go to Twitter.com and enter the #coedchat hashtag in the search box.
