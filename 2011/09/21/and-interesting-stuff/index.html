The figure shown here http://www.knowledgejump.com/web_pics/isd_robust.jpg is a good way to look at the model-as a circle vs as linear. Of course, the whole topic of ADDIE has been extremely polarizing as can be seen in this 2009 debate: http://goo.gl/oAlp5
