# So, I was doing some prototyping yesterday and I think as a result I have fallen in love with the Photo Stream part…

So, I was doing some prototyping yesterday and I think as a result I have fallen in love with the Photo Stream part of iCloud. I would draw something up on the iPad (in Adobe Ideas) and then take a screenshot. Then, a few seconds later, I could drag the image from iPhoto to my prototype I was working on in Keynote.

There is some truth in the advertising. It just worked.
