# At first I thought this was a little silly to put on my facebook profile, but then I realized that I really am not…

![](https://i0.wp.com/learningischange.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/makefbpic.png?w=1225)

At first I thought this was a little silly to put on my facebook profile, but then I realized that I really am not all that interested in people contacting me on Facebook. Why not state your preference for connecting to a network I want to be a part of helping to co-create:  [http://turhan.me/+me/index.php](http://turhan.me/+me/index.php)

So, as I said in a comment earlier today. It isn’t that Google+ is the answer. It’s just that Facebook has never solved a problem I ever had. Twitter has. So far, Google+ has as well.
