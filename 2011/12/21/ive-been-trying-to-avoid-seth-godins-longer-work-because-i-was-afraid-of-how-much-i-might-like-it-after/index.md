# I’ve been trying to avoid +Seth Godin’s longer work because I was afraid of how much I might like it. After…

I’ve been trying to avoid +Seth Godin’s longer work because I was afraid of how much I might like it. After listening to two audio books, I know I was right to fear his words. Now the changes I’ve been attempting are better defined and better supported. Thanks for that, Seth. Listening and not acting is so hard.
