# DCSD Conversations: A new beginning

In an effort to have some of the conversations that I engage in regularly on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/) and in the Edublogosphere within my home [School District](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_district), I have started having a semi-regular conversation with a few folks in [Douglas County](http://maps.google.com/maps?ll=43.29,-123.18&spn=1.0,1.0&q=43.29,-123.18%20%28Douglas%20County%2C%20Oregon%29&t=h) about [21st century](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_century) learning in our district. We have decided to make it into a [video podcast](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_podcast) that is hosted on our server, but is available through itunes.

[Here is the iTunes link.](http://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewPodcast?id=300840077)

I will also be crossposting the podcasts as a part of the Learning is Change podcasts, so here are the show notes from the second edition of this [podcast](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podcast):

We had a great time today talking about 21st century learning. The following are questions that we considered during this podcast:

- How can we best respond to the challenges and opportunities of the 21st century?

- How can we help empower today’s learners and teachers?

- What are the alternatives to schooling as we know it?

- What does 21st century learning practice actually look like (in a “real” classroom)?

- How can I contribute to transforming our [educational](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education) organizations?
