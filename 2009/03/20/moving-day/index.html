Moving Day!

March 20, 2009 103 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff
Image by leff via Flickr

I can only say this about a few days in my life, like the day when my
daughter was born or the day that put together my first 25 page paper
(all in one day), but today I am more tired than when I teach.

This says something about both the way that I pack and the way that I
teach. While I am a lousy packer, I believe that if you aren’t
totally exhausted after a full day of teaching and thinking with
kids, you aren’t doing it right.

Anyway, we are moved. Not unpacked, but moved.
