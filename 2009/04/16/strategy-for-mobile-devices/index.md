# Strategy for Mobile Devices

[![this is a jailbroken 1.1.](http://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/8/86/Jailbroken_ipod_touch_1.1.1_firmware.JPG/200px-Jailbroken_ipod_touch_1.1.1_firmware.JPG)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:Jailbroken_ipod_touch_1.1.1_firmware.JPG) Image via [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:Jailbroken_ipod_touch_1.1.1_firmware.JPG)

Our district is starting to consider using student smart phones as a part of our overall device strategy. As we continue to think about leveraging this growing group of learning opportunities, I have gathered three ideas that I think are worth putting on the table (and not just our district table):

- [This is an amazing list of iphone/ ipod touch apps that are split out by learning discipline](http://web.me.com/dowd/Touch/Welcome.html). There is more than enough there to do a pilot in high schools and middle schools.
- I have used [Textmarks.com](http://www.textmarks.com/animalfarm) and [Textthemob](http://textthemob.com/print/318)and a twitter backchannel (through texting, smart phones and the web) to great effect. Backchanneling should be a part of much of the PD and teaching practice in the future. (Putting texting into an assessment system isn’t quite ready yet, but I think it is going in that direction… it looks like you can already use sms for forums and communication via moodle: [http://www.moodletxt.co.uk](http://www.moodletxt.co.uk/))
- This video is a great example of what [the future may look like in terms of mobile devices.](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TLCTpX3tJEQ) It is youtube, so you will have to access it somewhere other than on a school network (depending on the school).
- I also think that [this blog](http://www.cellphonesinlearning.com/) is pretty much the coolest thing on the topic of cell phones in the classroom. Nice tools and a great focus on learning.

Let me know what other resources you are looking at in order to ensure that we can leverage these devices more in the future. And more than that, so we can actually have a strategy for how to do it.
