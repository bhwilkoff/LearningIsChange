# Blogs as Continuing Professional Development

[![Loch Tummel in Perth and Kinross.](http://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/Queens_View_-_Loch_Tummel.jpg/300px-Queens_View_-_Loch_Tummel.jpg)](http://commons.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:Queens_View_-_Loch_Tummel.jpg) Image via [Wikipedia](http://commons.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:Queens_View_-_Loch_Tummel.jpg)

I got a message on twitter that 5 posts from this blog are now [available to anyone who is a part of Continuing Professional Development in Scotland](http://www.ltscotland.org.uk/cpdscotland/cpdfind/searchcpd/oppdetails.asp?inst=3499). It appears as though this means that people will be able to read my blog for some kind of credit.

While I am intrigued that my writing may make its way into any type of curriculum, it makes me think a whole lot more about how we package learning materials from others.

Can we make anything into curriculum? Can we offer credit for any type of academic pursuit?

I think that the kind of work that brings outside resources into the realm of canonized professional development is just wonderful. But, since they are linking to me, perhaps I am little biased.
