# Honest with myself…

[![An example of a social network diagram.](http://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/Social-network.svg/300px-Social-network.svg.png)](http://commons.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:Social-network.svg) Image via [Wikipedia](http://commons.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:Social-network.svg)

Where I to be totally honest with myself:

- I would stop whatever I am doing and find the nearest classroom to teach in.
- I would continually say to those that ask for my advice on such matters that I have never taken nor taught an online-only course.
- I would admit that I care about the number of followers I have on twitter for reasons that I do not completely understand.
- I would realize that not having read an entire novel in over a year is not okay with me.
- I would tell everyone that I don’t have the ability to follow through on all of the projects to which I have currently committed myself.
