# Question 197 of 365: Do laughs echo?

Only if the words are true and the hope of the next joke is within
every moment.

The funny bounces around the pointed ceiling and whips us up into a
frienzy. We like hearing it cascade and end up in our throats and
ears. Yes. That’s it.
