Presentations:

- [Liquid Scenarios](http://liquidscenarios.com/) – A single button to help people valuate a single company… This allows us to know exactly how much a company is worth. There is no liquid assets for a company that isn’t sold. [The Demo for this company was not working… couldn’t figure out how to work vista]
- [Team Snap](http://www.teamsnap.com/) – Managing your team online. Everyone does teams and coaching management wrong… they do it right, apparently. I like the idea of managing logistics with software to concentrate on human interactions. “People pay for what we do” (that must be nice)
- [CSIA](http://coloradotechnology.org/) – Should the [Colorado Office of state planning and budgeting put a sales tax on software](http://community.coloradotechnology.org/news/34456/URGENT--Complicated-New-Sales-Taxes-Proposed-for-Software-Industry-and-IT-Operations-in-Colorado.htm)? A really fantastic speaker, truly knowledgeable about sales tax for physical objects vs. services and online purchases.
- [Easy Insight](http://www.easy-insight.com/)– Business Intelligence, there is an actual demo (Such a great change). Get your data from almost anywhere (Google Analytics, Basecamp, etc.) Yeah! Lets build reports. Cool visualization!
- [Ubisense](http://www.ubisense.net/en) – Precise Real Time Location Systems. Let’s track people indoors using sensors. They use [Ultrawide Band for doing this tracking.](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra-wideband)And Cow detection. I’m not sure it gets any better than that.
