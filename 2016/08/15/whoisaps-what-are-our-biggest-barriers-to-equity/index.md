# #WHOISaps: What are our biggest barriers to equity?

[Mark Calhoun](https://twitter.com/The_Mighty74), Director of MTSS for Aurora Public Schools, discusses issues of equity, the accountability era, and the ways in which we can break down silos across schools and in central office. He also dives into the pressure of modern public education and the way in which we take things “off the plate” of teachers.
