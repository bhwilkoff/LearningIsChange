What I'm Using: Free (amazing) Video Stock Footage

April 30, 2014 151 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

I really had no idea that there was this great treasure trove of wonderful stock footage for video projects. There are tons of things that could be used in the classroom or for educational remixing. Let me know if you are using them for any digital storytelling projects, as well. I’m always game to see great examples of that.

Download Free HD Stock Footage · XStockvideo.com

Is everything for free? We have a loth of footage available for you in FULL HD. Browse the site and start downloading them. All our footage in web resolution 960×540 is also available for free to use in a ‘Free to view video project’ (promotional, event, educational, client, youtube without ads). If you want to use the footage for commercial purposes (a film or television production, commercial app, game, youtube with adds or other software) you can buy an extended License for a small fee.
