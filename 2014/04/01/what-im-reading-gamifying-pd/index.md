# What I'm Reading: Gamifying PD

This is a wonderful summary of how to Gamify PD. It is inspired by one of my favorite experts on the subject, [Philip Vinogradov](https://plus.google.com/u/0/+PhilipVinogradov/about). I would love to learn/think more about how “leader boards” affect a group of adults that aren’t used to them within more traditional games. Are they helpful or hurtful to the Professional Learning environment.

[How to Gamify Professional Development in Your School – A.J. Juliani](http://ajjuliani.com/gamify-professional-development-school)

> Sometimes we act like professional development is hard. It doesn’t have to be. Sometimes we treat professional development like a chore. It should be fun, engaging, and wanted.
