# What I'm Learning: Jugaad

I’ve had this open tab on chrome for three weeks trying to figure out why I can’t close it. I think I don’t want to close it because I want to be capable of a Jugaad for education. I want us all to be capable of it, actually.

[Jugaad – Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jugaad)

> Jugaad is a colloquial Hindi word that can mean an innovative fix or a simple work-around,[1] sometimes pejoratively used for solutions that bend rules, or a resource that can be used as such, or a person who can solve a complicated issue.
