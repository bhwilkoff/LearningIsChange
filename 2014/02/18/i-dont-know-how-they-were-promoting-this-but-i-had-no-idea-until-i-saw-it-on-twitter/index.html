I don’t know how they were promoting this, but I had no idea until I saw it on twitter.

I’m kind of thinking that #edcampdenver should take over #coedchat, so that CDE understands just how powerful this community is.

Anyone want to start posting a whole bunch of questions and ideas on #nextgenlearning for #coedchat next week?

The Next Gen and Blended & Online Learning team at CDE is a small but mighty group of really amazing educators and advocates, but they need our help showing just how powerful and important these issues are because they can’t initiate and sustain this change alone.

You can post anytime before Monday at 4, and can participate during the discussion by following & using those hashtags 4-4:45pm. So go throw a question or comment out there, it’s fun!
