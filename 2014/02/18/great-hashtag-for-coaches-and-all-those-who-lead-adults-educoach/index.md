# Great hashtag for coaches (and all those who lead adults): #EduCoach

I’m not sure why I hadn’t seen this before, but I think this is a great hashtag and chat that seem to be providing a lot of great resources for folks around the world. I really like their wiki as well.

[EduCoach – home](http://educoachchat.wikispaces.com/)

> The #educoach Chat is held each Wednesday evening at 9:00 – 10:00 PM CST. Please use the #educoach hashtag whenever you have something to share with the group.
