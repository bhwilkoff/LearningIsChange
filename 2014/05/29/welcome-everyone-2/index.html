Welcome Everyone!

May 29, 2014 86 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

Welcome Everyone!

If you haven’t submitted a proposal, go ahead and do so right now.

However, if you have already submitted your proposal, finish the process by posting the link to it within this community. Based upon the feedback within your proposal (comments, questions, etc.) and the number of +1’s and reshares your proposal receives within this community, you will be chosen to be a part of Project Cam Opener for the 2014-2015 school year.

I’m looking forward to learning a lot from each of you.

    You vote with your +1’s and comments. The proposals with more of them will be more likely to receive all of the items they have requested.

    The proposals will also be looked at by school and district leaders in the next round, but we are keeping it to fellow teachers in this one.
