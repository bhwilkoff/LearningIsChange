What I'm Using: UnWhiteboard

May 14, 2014 68 words · 1 min read by Ben Wilkoff

This is a really simple hack that seemingly has absolutely no downside. It allows you to send a picture of your whiteboard notes to an email address and then receive a PDF of them back. Very nice. (The email address is please@make.unwhiteboard.com in case you don’t want to click through.)
