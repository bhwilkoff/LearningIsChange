# What I'm Using: UnWhiteboard

This is a really simple hack that seemingly has absolutely no downside. It allows you to send a picture of your whiteboard notes to an email address and then receive a PDF of them back. Very nice. (The email address is [please@make.unwhiteboard.com](mailto:please@make.unwhiteboard.com) in case you don’t want to click through.)

[Generate pdf slide deck from whiteboard photos – unwhiteboard.com](http://www.unwhiteboard.com/)

> Turn your whiteboard photos into clean legible pdf documents
