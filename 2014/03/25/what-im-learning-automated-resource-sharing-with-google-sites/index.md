# What I'm Learning: Automated Resource Sharing with Google Sites

I cannot take any of the credit for the amazing work that [John Calvert](https://plus.google.com/+JohnCalvert) has done. He is truly inspirational in all that he is creating for his school district. I just wanted to take a moment and bask in the glow that is this one stop shop of resource sharing. It automatically tags, sorts, and creates pages for resources shared in their schools. There is more than a little bit of “Wow” going on here. If that isn’t enough, you should look at the [other areas of their support site. They are wonderful.](https://sites.google.com/a/elem.scarsdaleschools.net/stc/)
