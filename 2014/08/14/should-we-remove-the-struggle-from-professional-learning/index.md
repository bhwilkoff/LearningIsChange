# Should We Remove The Struggle From Professional Learning?

In this podcast episode I discuss whether or not it is important for us to remove struggle from professional learning experiences. Do you have any thoughts? Should we be taking out steps and making things easier for people to learn, or does this rob them of their ability to learn and to retain that learning?
