# I wanted to share this out to all of my circles.

I wanted to share this out to all of my circles. If you haven’t seen The Fellowship of Open Spokes project, I encourage you to check it out for version 2.0!

Come reflect on your practice in our collaborative YouTube channel. We’ve been going strong for over a year now.
