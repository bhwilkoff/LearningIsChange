    Storytelling is and has been one of the everlasting mediums for exchanging information because it has plot arc. People want to listen to engaging stories because they can empathize with the protagonist. They can take a virtual journey vicariously through time and space and come out of the experience all the better for it. I would love to have you and your associates visit my SEEd Google+ community to see what I am doing to promote digital storytelling through students using Moviestorm 3D animation software. Once you have seen what we are doing there, perhaps you might want to “Be Your Story”!
