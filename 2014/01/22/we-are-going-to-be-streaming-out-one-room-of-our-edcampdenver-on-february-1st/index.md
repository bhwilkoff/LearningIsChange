# We are going to be streaming out one room of our #EdCampDenver on February 1st.

We are going to be streaming out one room of our #EdCampDenver on February 1st. Even if you aren’t in the area, you can watch!

Originally shared by edcamp Denver

This event goes all day!

This is the live streaming portion of EdCamp Denver at KCAA on February 1st. We are looking to connect with folks who can’t make it to the EdCamp locally or who are at another EdCamp happening in, Madison, Magnet,  Savannah or Round Rock ISD.
