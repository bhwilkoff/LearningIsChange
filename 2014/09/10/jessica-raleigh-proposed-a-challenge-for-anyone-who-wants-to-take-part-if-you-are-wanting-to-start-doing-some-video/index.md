# Jessica Raleigh proposed a challenge for anyone who wants to take part. If you are wanting to start doing some video…

Jessica Raleigh proposed a challenge for anyone who wants to take part. If you are wanting to start doing some video blogging, here are a couple of great questions to get you started:

☑What’s in your desk drawer, and what can you infer from those contents?

☑Share five random facts about yourself. Share four things from your bucket list. Share three things that you hope for this year, as a “person” or an educator. Share two things that have made you laugh or cry as an educator. Share one thing you wish more people knew about you.

☑Write your goals for the school year. Be as specific or abstract as you’d like to be!

☑Discuss one “observation” area that you would like to improve on for your teacher evaluation.

☑Explain: What does a good mentor “do”?

☑Who was or is your most inspirational colleague, and why?

Again, this is just some fun ideas to get to know one another and start the process of reflection through video. Looking forward to “seeing” you soon!
