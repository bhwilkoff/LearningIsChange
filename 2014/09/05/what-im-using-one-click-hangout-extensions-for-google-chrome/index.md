# What I'm Using: One Click Hangout Extensions for Google Chrome!

I have needed these things SO MUCH in the last few months. The ability to start broadcasting directly to your youtube channel with a single click is pretty awesome.

[One Click Google Hangout – Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/one-click-google-hangout/aokjakdncnbbfhhammcdkbblmcglpobn)

> Presenting: “THE ONE CLICK GOOGLE HANGOUT BUTTON” After installing this extension you are just ONE click away from hanging out! Here are the main features: – Start a hangout right away, no more waiting and clicking through endless menus – Once the hangout is started, the hangout url is copied to your clipboard

[One Click Google On-Air Hangout – Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/one-click-google-on-air-h/hjdfingjhknmknmikpijefojjegnkmjk)

> Presenting: “THE ONE CLICK ON-AIR GOOGLE HANGOUT BUTTON” After installing this extension you are just ONE click away from hanging out! Here are the main features: – Start a hangout right away, no more waiting and clicking through endless menus – Once the hangout is started, the hangout url is copied to your clipboard
