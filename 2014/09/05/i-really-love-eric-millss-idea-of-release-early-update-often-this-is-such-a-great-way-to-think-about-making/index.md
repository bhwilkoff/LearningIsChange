# I really love Eric Mills’s idea of “release early, update often.” This is such a great way to think about making…

I really love Eric Mills’s idea of “release early, update often.” This is such a great way to think about making videos and collaborating with others online. Anything you create for this project does not have to be perfect. Please share above all else.

if you have made a video already, share it with us. If you have tried out hangouts, share your experience. If your kids have gotten excited about the new tools in the classroom, share what has made them excited. This community is here to support you.
