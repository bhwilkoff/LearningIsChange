# We have an opportunity for a number of us  to be a part of the Classroom Video Challenge, which was launched by the…

We have an opportunity for a number of us  to be a part of the Classroom Video Challenge, which was launched by the Gates Foundation recently.

This is going to be an ongoing initiative that we can help support with our video work, but for the moment, we are looking to identify 1 teacher and 1 support partner (coach, principal, etc.) to go to a convening in San Fran on Aug 2nd and to help push the thinking of this group.

Would you take a look at this PDF and let me know if you are interested in the next couple of days, as we need to get our names in to take part. I think this is heavily aligned to our project, but we don’t all need to take part.

I’m so excited to work with you all. Thank you so much for your collaboration. Over 250 ratings thus far on your proposals!
