# We are heading into the final stretch of the proposal rating process, and thus far we have had over 375 ratings!

We are heading into the final stretch of the proposal rating process, and thus far we have had over 375 ratings! We are down to the last few days, and only a few have not received the 3 ratings each that I would like to see. This doc is the list of folks who have not received enough ratings, and I wanted to ask this community to help pitch in and rate the last few so that everyone gets a fair shake at feedback. Go team!
